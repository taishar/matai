What should the badge say?

Same 44-day range — four ways to label it

A

Days only

Clean and direct. Always one number — no conversion, no ambiguity.

📅 44 days
B

Weeks + days

Broken down into human-friendly units. Easier to grasp for longer ranges.

📅 6 weeks, 2 days
C

Both

Exact days plus the human breakdown. Most informative — slightly longer.

📅 44 days · 6 weeks, 2 days
D

Adaptive

Short ranges show days only ("5 days"), longer ranges automatically switch to the breakdown ("6 weeks, 2 days").

📅 5 days 📅 6 weeks, 2 days
